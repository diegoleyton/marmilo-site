Why MarMilo?

Every child grows differently.

Some children need a gentle reminder. Others need more support. Some routines come naturally, and others take more time.

MarMilo was created to help families build positive habits in a way that adapts to each child.

Instead of focusing on mistakes, MarMilo helps make progress visible. Instead of constant negotiation, it helps families build calmer routines.

And instead of measuring success by screen time, MarMilo is designed to support stronger habits, greater independence, and more positive family moments.

Technology should strengthen family life, not compete with it.

MarMilo combines family routines, positive reinforcement, and friendly companions into one shared experience.

How MarMilo Works

One ecosystem. Built around your child.

MarMilo is a connected ecosystem designed to help children build positive habits across different parts of their lives.

At its core, MarMilo Parents and MarMilo Kids connect families around the behaviors they want to encourage, making progress visible and meaningful for each child.

Parents

  • Create the Expected Behaviors you'd like to encourage.
  • Record positive behaviors as they happen.
  • Define how challenging each behavior is for each child.
  • Create meaningful Incentives.
  • Celebrate progress together.
Open MarMilo Parents

Kids

  • Complete everyday routines.
  • Earn MarMilo Stars.
  • Care for your companion.
  • Redeem the Incentives you've chosen using your earned MarMilo Stars.
  • Celebrate every small success along the way.

An ecosystem that grows with them

Every child is different - and the habits they need support with are different too.

That's why MarMilo is growing into a collection of connected experiences that families can choose from based on each child's age, interests, needs, and the habits they want to encourage.

For one child, that might mean MarMilo Brush, helping make independent tooth brushing part of their everyday routine.

For another, it might mean MarMilo Maths, an experience we're currently developing to help children explore mathematics while building something equally important: the habit of practicing math.

Because the goal isn't simply to get the right answer. It's to explore, practice, make mistakes, try again - and want to come back tomorrow.

Each experience connects back to the same MarMilo ecosystem, so the progress children make becomes part of the encouragement and achievements they already share with their families.

As children grow, families can choose the experiences that grow with them.

Different children. Different habits. Different experiences. One connected MarMilo.

Our Story

Built for real families.

MarMilo didn't begin as a startup idea. It began at home.

I'm Diego, a software engineer and father of two. After more than 14 years building interactive products, including seven years at Meta, I wanted to build the tool our own family wished we had.

Like many families, we're still learning every day. Some routines have become easier, while others continue to challenge us.

Today, MarMilo is growing alongside other real families whose stories and feedback continue shaping every new version.

FAQ

A few quick answers.

Is MarMilo free?

Yes. MarMilo Parents and MarMilo Kids are currently available to use at no cost.

Who is MarMilo designed for?

MarMilo is currently designed primarily for children between 3 and 10 years old, although every family is different.

Do I need both apps?

Not necessarily. Parents can set everything up in MarMilo Parents, and MarMilo Kids is there for the child experience. They work best together, but you can start with what fits your family.

Where can I use MarMilo?

MarMilo is available on iPhone, Android, and in your browser using the links on this page.

Can I install MarMilo on Android?

Yes. We currently have Android test builds available from a shared download link. Because they are still in testing, Android may ask you to allow app installs from your browser or files app before you can install them.
